# About

This site is a compendium of **positive geometries**, with an emphasis
on the polytopal examples: their definitions, canonical forms, and
combinatorics. It is maintained by Tomasz Lukowski.

## Why this exists

Positive geometries give a single, uniform language for objects that
otherwise look unrelated — convex polytopes, the amplituhedron, the
kinematic associahedron — by characterizing each through a canonical
differential form fixed entirely by its boundary structure (see
[Positive geometries](theory/positive-geometries.md)). This site collects
the polytopal examples in one place: what each one is, its f-vector and
generating functions, and its canonical form, worked out consistently
across families so they can be compared directly (see the
[data page](data/f-vector-tables.md)).

This is a rebuild of a first attempt at the same idea from 2021. That
version stayed close to four examples (simplex, hypercube, associahedron,
permutohedron); this one keeps those, fills in the theory that was
missing, adds more of the catalog (the cross-polytope, hypersimplex,
cyclic polytope, and the generalized-permutohedron picture that ties them
together), and backs every family with a
[Jupyter notebook](notebooks.md) that verifies its claims computationally
rather than asking you to take them on faith.
